The strategic position

The operator’s guide to school-safety readiness.

Jorge does not need to out-tacticalize former federal agents or large security firms. The stronger category is the operator who can connect regulations, governance, national standards, budgets, and implementation.

01 · Translate

What is required?

Start with primary sources. Separate statute, agency guidance, standards, and judgment so leaders know what they are dealing with.

02 · Operationalize

What does “done” look like?

Turn requirements into owners, evidence, timelines, routines, training, reporting, and corrective-action systems.

03 · Improve

What should happen next?

Move beyond binary compliance. Use findings and data to prioritize the next best safety investment and build continuous improvement into governance.

Proposed advisory architecture

Assess → prioritize → implement → verify.

The commercial model should be diagnostic first, implementation second, continuous improvement third. Every engagement should be vendor-neutral, evidence-based, and explicit about scope and confidentiality.
